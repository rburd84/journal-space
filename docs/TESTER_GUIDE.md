# Stillspace — Tester User Guide (Draft)

**Audience:** Invited private-beta testers. **Status:** Pre-launch draft; verify screens and wording in hosted acceptance testing before distribution.

## Welcome
Stillspace is a private place to organize your writing into journals. During the invitation-only beta, only approved accounts can access the app. You can write drafts, publish entries, and mark favorites. **Published** is an organization status inside your own account; it does not publish your writing publicly.

**Important:** Deleting an entry is permanent. Stillspace does not currently offer a trash folder or 30-day recovery. Avoid entering information you cannot afford to lose during the beta.

## 1. Sign in
1. Open the Stillspace website provided in your invitation (the final URL will be shared after launch approval).
2. Use the email address that was invited.
3. Follow the Clerk sign-in instructions on screen. Available verification methods depend on the configured Clerk instance.
4. If you see an access or invitation error, contact the beta administrator rather than creating a different account.

## 2. Create a journal
1. Select **Journals** from the navigation.
2. Choose the create-journal action.
3. Enter a journal title and choose a journal type if prompted.
4. Save the journal. Your journal appears in the journal list.

## 3. Write an entry
1. Open a journal, or choose the new-entry action from the navigation.
2. If you started outside a journal, select the journal to write in.
3. Enter a title and your writing.
4. Select **Save Draft**. Wait for confirmation before navigating away.
5. You may select **Publish** after the entry is saved. This changes its status; it does not make the entry public.

**Saving is manual:** Do not assume unfinished edits have been autosaved.

## 4. Find, read, and edit writing
- Use **Entries** to browse writing, including journal/status filtering where available.
- Open an entry to read it.
- Select **Edit Entry** to change its title or content, then save your changes.
- Use the star/**Favorite** control to mark or unmark an entry.

## 5. Manage journals
- In **Journals**, open the three-dot menu for a journal to **Rename**, **Archive**, or **Restore**.
- Archived journals can be restored. Archiving is not the same as deleting entries.

## 6. Delete an entry — permanent
1. Open the entry.
2. Select **Delete Entry**.
3. Read the confirmation and proceed only if you are sure.

There is currently no in-app undo or Recently Deleted recovery. Contact the administrator promptly if you delete something by mistake, but recovery cannot be promised.

## 7. Troubleshooting and feedback
- If the app reports a session problem, try the on-screen retry action or sign in again.
- If an invitation is rejected, confirm that you used the invited email.
- If saving fails, preserve your text before refreshing the page.
- For a bug report, include the page, approximate time, device/browser, and steps to reproduce. **Do not send private journal text or screenshots containing sensitive writing** unless you explicitly choose to.
- Use the feedback contact address provided with your invitation; it is not yet finalized in this draft.

## Beta limitations
This is a limited test, not a general public release. Features such as a 30-day Recently Deleted area, data export, and account self-service deletion are not documented as available. Your administrator will communicate beta dates, privacy terms, and support details before launch.
